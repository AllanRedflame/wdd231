Pacific Pathfinders

Name: Pacific Pathfinders. I wanted to pick a location that was easily recognizable, so I chose Seattle. The pacific opens it up to include a larger area. Also, I'm fond of alliteration.

Purpose: The purpose of this website will be to help to plan travel by providing information about various travel locations.

Scenarios: Where are the best places to travel for this time of year? What do I need to know before traveling to a specific destination?

Colors:
#A6e1fa Light blue. Main color to be used throughout the site. Header, footer, main content
#001c55 Dark blue. Primarily an accent. Borders, underlines, maybe to color the page background.
#0f8e8f Light green. I may also use this to go along with the darker accents.

Fonts: Archivo Black for titles and headings. Roboto for paragraphs and blocks of text.
